# God of War Adventure Game - Story Plan

## Objective
Create a web-based choose-your-own-adventure game where the player takes the role of Kratos. 
The story branches based on player choices and leads to multiple endings.

---

## Story Overview
You are **Kratos**, the God of War. Your journey begins in **Midgard**, where danger lurks everywhere.  
Your choices will determine the path you take and the outcome of your adventure.

### Paths
There are **3 main paths** in this adventure:

1. **Forest Battle Path**  
2. **Bridge of the Dead Path**  
3. **Final Battle Path**

---

## Path 1: Forest Battle

### Decision Point 1
- **Option A:** Fight the Draugr head-on  
- **Option B:** Sneak past them  

### Outcomes
- **Fight:** Kratos slays Draugr but loses health. Atreus is proud. Finds a chest with Spartan Rage.  
- **Sneak:** Avoids fight, but Atreus doubts Kratos’ courage. Later ambushed by more enemies.

---

## Path 2: Bridge of the Dead

### Decision Point 1
- **Option A:** Pay the ferryman with a rune  
- **Option B:** Refuse and fight  

### Outcomes
- **Pay:** Safely crosses to Helheim entrance  
- **Fight:** Battles a horde of Draugr. Atreus is captured → must rescue him.

---

## Path 3: Final Battle

### Decision Point 1
- **Option A:** Confront Baldur immediately  
- **Option B:** Wait and set a trap  

### Outcomes
- **Confront:** Intense boss fight. Player can **win or lose**  
- **Trap:** Baldur is weakened → easier fight → leads to victory

---

## Endings
1. **Victory:** Kratos and Atreus survive, preparing for Ragnarok ⚡  
2. **Defeat:** Baldur kills Kratos 💀  
3. **Journey continues:** Story progresses for another adventure 🔥
